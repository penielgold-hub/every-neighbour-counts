**Purpose:** This form helps the initiative receive, verify and assess requests for education-related support. Completing the form does not guarantee assistance. All support depends on verification, available resources and approval.

**Important privacy notice:** A parent or legal guardian should complete this form for a child under 18. Collect only information needed to assess the request. Do not send this form or supporting documents to a public WhatsApp group or social media page. Do not request NIN, BVN, bank PINs, passwords or one-time passcodes.

## 2. Assessment considerations

Reviewers should consider:

- Is the expense education-related, necessary and within the programme's scope?
- Is the amount reasonable and supported by reliable evidence?
- Is the deadline genuine and confirmed?
- Is the student at risk of missing school, an examination or another important educational step?
- Has the family explored reasonable school-based or other support?
- Is there another confirmed source of payment for the same expense?
- Can the initiative meet all or part of the need without making an unsustainable commitment?
- Have privacy, consent and safeguarding requirements been followed?

# Handling and safeguarding checklist

Before closing an application, the responsible volunteer should confirm:

- [ ] The parent/guardian or adult applicant understood the purpose of the form.
- [ ] Only necessary information was collected.
- [ ] Any school or examination verification was conducted with appropriate consent.
- [ ] No sensitive identity credentials, passwords, PINs or OTPs were requested.
- [ ] The application and evidence are stored securely with restricted access.
- [ ] The decision and any payment are supported by records.
- [ ] Public reporting, if any, uses anonymised or aggregated information.
- [ ] The student and family were treated respectfully and without discrimination.

**Record retention:** Keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for assessment, delivery, accountability or a legitimate legal requirement. Establish a retention and secure-deletion procedure before collecting applications at scale.
