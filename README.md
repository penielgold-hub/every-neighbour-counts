# EVERY NEIGHBOUR COUNTS

**Real People. Real Help. Measurable Impact.**

*Your family first. Your neighbour next. Nobody left behind.*

Every Neighbour Counts is a grassroots, volunteer-led community-support initiative. We encourage individuals to identify vulnerable people within their immediate families and neighbourhoods, understand their actual needs, offer practical help where possible, and connect them with legitimate sources of assistance and opportunity.

Our starting point is deliberately local: **begin with the people closest to you.**

## Why this initiative exists

Elderly people, widows, persons with disabilities, unemployed individuals and struggling households may face barriers to information, essential services, digital access, healthcare, education, employment and social support. Some of these people are already part of our families and neighbourhoods.

We believe meaningful community support can begin with an ordinary person giving time, knowledge, skills, practical help or a useful connection.

## Our mission

To empower individuals to identify vulnerable people within their families and neighbourhoods, understand their needs, provide practical assistance where possible, and connect them with legitimate sources of support and opportunities.

## Our guiding principle

1. **Start with your family.** Check on elderly and vulnerable relatives.
2. **Reach your neighbour.** Extend the same concern to people living nearby.
3. **Understand the need.** Listen before deciding what help is appropriate.
4. **Take practical action.** Offer realistic help or make a responsible referral.
5. **Follow up.** Find out whether the assistance made a difference.
6. **Encourage another person.** Help the effort grow through trusted relationships.

## Areas of support

- Elderly assistance and digital inclusion.
- Support and referrals for vulnerable households.
- Access to verified public information and social assistance.
- Skills development, training and employment opportunities.
- Inclusion and accessibility for persons with disabilities.
- Connections to legitimate community, charitable and institutional services.

## How we work

WhatsApp will be our initial channel for education, volunteer coordination and sharing verified opportunities. This GitHub repository is the public record of our mission, guidance, campaigns, contributions and learning.

Volunteers are not expected to personally fund every need. Help may take the form of time, listening, guidance, practical assistance, skills or a connection to an appropriate service.

## Measuring impact

We will measure results by meaningful action, not by message volume or promises.

We aim to document, in privacy-respecting and aggregated form:

- People who received practical assistance.
- Households supported.
- Referrals made and referrals with confirmed outcomes.
- People connected to training, employment or essential services.
- Active volunteers and completed community activities.
- Problems addressed and lessons learned.

We will distinguish between an enquiry, a referral, assistance delivered and an outcome confirmed. We will not claim an outcome that has not been verified.

## Integrity and safeguarding

- Participation is voluntary and non-partisan.
- Treat every person with dignity; do not discriminate.
- Do not promise assistance that cannot be delivered.
- Do not charge beneficiaries for access to information or referrals.
- Do not impersonate government agencies or partner organisations.
- Verify opportunities and official instructions before sharing them.
- Collect only information genuinely needed for a specific, consented activity.
- Never publish NINs, BVNs, bank details, passwords, OTPs, private phone numbers or other sensitive beneficiary information.
- Obtain informed consent before sharing identifiable stories, photographs or testimonials.
- Refer safeguarding concerns to appropriate qualified services; volunteers must not investigate or expose people to further risk.

## Relationship to government programmes

We may explain and refer people to legitimate government interventions, including the Federal Government's NERSI ₦50,000 relief programme, where relevant. Such programmes are individual initiatives, not the entirety of Every Neighbour Counts.

We are an independent community initiative, not a government agency, payment provider or beneficiary-selection authority. We cannot enrol people into a government beneficiary list, guarantee eligibility or guarantee payment. Always consult the relevant official source for current requirements.

## Getting involved

You can begin without joining an organisation or making a financial contribution:

1. Check on an elderly or vulnerable person in your family.
2. Ask whether a nearby neighbour needs practical help.
3. Listen and agree on an appropriate next step.
4. Help directly if you safely can, or identify a legitimate service to contact.
5. Follow up respectfully.

Read [our founding concept](docs/founding-concept.md) for the full programme framework. Contributions and suggestions are welcome through GitHub issues and pull requests.
